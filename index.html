FAQ

Questions you might have.

If your question isn't here, send me a message and I'll answer it.

Ask me directly →
How does the website process work?

You tell me about your business, I send a project scope and quotation, I build the website for your review, and you can request the revisions included in your package. Once you approve the final version, I deliver it. The steps are set out in the How it works section.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the scope and on how quickly I receive your content and feedback. A one-page website is usually quicker than a multi-page one. I confirm a timeline in your quotation rather than promising one in advance.

What do I need to provide?

Your business details, the services you offer, your contact information and any logo, photos or examples you like. I can help structure the wording, but accurate information and images you have the right to use need to come from you.

What do the revision rounds include?

A revision round is one consolidated set of change requests to the agreed design and content. The Starter Website includes one round and the Business Website includes two. Changes beyond that, or work outside the agreed scope, are quoted separately. Revisions are not unlimited.

Are the domain name and hosting included?

No. Domain registration and any paid hosting or third-party services are separate costs that you pay directly to the provider. Many simple websites can be hosted on free or low-cost static hosting, and I can help publish your finished website to the host you choose.

How do payments work?

Payment terms are written into your quotation before work begins, typically a deposit to start and the balance before final delivery. I will confirm the amounts and accepted payment methods in the quotation.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes, through the Website Care plan, which covers minor content updates, basic website checks and support within agreed limits. It is not unlimited: larger changes, new pages or new features are quoted separately.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Send me the link and I will tell you what I think could be improved. I rebuild the front end as a clean, responsive static website. I don't migrate large platforms, databases or online stores.
